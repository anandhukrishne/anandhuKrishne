Privacy Policy — Weather.45

Last Updated: November 2026 • Developer: 45 Studios (Kurup Anandhukrishne Vinodkumar)

Weather.45 is designed with user privacy as a core principle.

1. No Personal Data Collection

Weather.45 does not require account registration and does not collect, store, or sell your name, email address, phone number, device identifiers, or precise GPS location.

2. Search Queries & Third-Party API

When you manually search for a city name, that query is sent over HTTPS to the Open-Meteo Geocoding and Weather Forecast API (open-meteo.com) solely to retrieve current temperature, humidity, and wind speed data. No personal identifiers are attached to these requests.

3. No Analytics or Ad Trackers

Weather.45 contains zero third-party advertising SDKs or behavioral tracking libraries.

4. Children’s Privacy

Weather.45 does not knowingly collect any personal information from children under 13.

5. Contact

For privacy inquiries, contact 45 Studios via anandhukrishne.tech.
